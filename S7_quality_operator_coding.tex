\section*{Quality appraisal}
The manuscript specifies ten items scored 0--2, with a maximum of 20 and an eligibility threshold of 16/20 for the deep critical corpus. The operational anchors below make the rubric reproducible. They do not substitute for the original assessor decisions, which were not present in the supplied archive.

\section*{Mechanism-coding representation}
An optimizer is represented as
\[
\mathcal{A}=(\mathcal{S},\mathcal{O}_{E},\mathcal{O}_{X},\pi_t,\mathcal{M},\mathcal{C}),
\]
where $\mathcal{S}$ is maintained state, $\mathcal{O}_{E}$ exploration/diversification, $\mathcal{O}_{X}$ exploitation/intensification, $\pi_t$ the activation/adaptation policy, $\mathcal{M}$ memory/information exchange and $\mathcal{C}$ selection/acceptance/constraint handling.

Four recurring primitives are
\begin{align*}
\mathcal{O}_{D}:~\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t+1}&=\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+a_t(\mathbf{x}_{j}^{t}-\mathbf{x}_{k}^{t}),\\
\mathcal{O}_{A}:~\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t+1}&=\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+b_t(\mathbf{x}_{\star}^{t}-\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}),\\
\mathcal{O}_{R}:~\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t+1}&=\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\sigma_t\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}_{i}^{t},\\
\mathcal{O}_{I}:~\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t+1}&=\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}+\sum_{j\in\mathcal{N}_i}w_{ij}^{t}(\mathbf{x}_{j}^{t}-\mathbf{x}_{i}^{t}).
\end{align*}
