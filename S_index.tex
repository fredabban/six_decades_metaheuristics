\section*{Controlling numerical identities}
This package follows the latest attached manuscript: 887 historical catalogue entries; 4,696 identified records; 3,810 duplicate/duplicate-version instances removed; 886 deduplicated reports screened; 824 reports not retained in the deep critical corpus; and 62 retained studies. The benchmark/application subsets are 42 rank-compatible, 35 fixed-budget, 25 dimensional-scaling and 18 real-world reports, with overlap permitted.

\section*{Non-fabrication rule}
Several record-level source artefacts are explicitly stated in the manuscript to be unavailable: original database-export timestamps, the full 824-row exclusion ledger, paired screening decisions, the complete RP1--RP62 Q1--Q10 assessor matrix and the fully populated EXP-level quantitative extraction file. The supplements therefore make those limitations auditable and use ``not recoverable from supplied archive'' as a data-status statement rather than inventing rows, dates, scores or effect estimates.
