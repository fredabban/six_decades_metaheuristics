\section*{Required experiment-level data dictionary}
\begin{longtable}{P{4.0cm}P{10.2cm}}

Field & Definition\\
\midrule\endfirsthead
\toprule Field & Definition\\\midrule\endhead
\texttt{EXP\_id} & Stable identifier for one benchmark/application comparison.\\
\texttt{STU\_id} & Study-level identifier linking repeated experiments from the same investigation.\\
\texttt{REP\_id} & Bibliographic report containing the observation.\\
\texttt{ALG\_id} & Stable algorithm entity identifier.\\
\texttt{benchmark} & Benchmark suite or named real-world application.\\
\texttt{function} & Function/problem identifier within the suite, if applicable.\\
\texttt{dimension} & Decision-space dimension or problem size.\\
\texttt{objective\_count} & Number of objectives for multi-/many-objective experiments.\\
\texttt{budget} & Function-evaluation budget or explicit stopping rule.\\
\texttt{run\_count} & Number of independent runs supporting the reported statistic.\\
\texttt{comparator} & Comparator algorithm and configuration.\\
\texttt{reported\_statistic} & Mean, median, best, rank, error, hypervolume, IGD, runtime, or other reported outcome.\\
\texttt{uncertainty} & Standard deviation, interquartile range, confidence interval, or other uncertainty measure where reported.\\
\texttt{direction} & Within-study comparison code: $+1$ better, $0$ tie/not distinguishable, $-1$ worse after orienting the metric.\\
\texttt{hardware\_software} & Hardware/software details for runtime comparisons.\\
\texttt{source\_locator} & Exact source page and table/figure number.\\
\texttt{transformation} & Any normalization, digitization, orientation, or derived calculation applied by the review.\\

\section*{Traceability status}
The supplied manuscript explicitly states that the fully populated experiment-level quantitative extraction sheet was unavailable for the final evidence audit. Consequently, this supplement does not invent EXP rows, family-level ranks, pooled errors, degradation exponents, or cross-study $p$-values. Quantitative claims in the manuscript are restricted accordingly. The four subset sizes above are retained as candidate evidence-set counts only.

\section*{Convergence-curve rule}
A convergence figure is admissible only if its source study, benchmark, dimension, evaluation grid, run count, aggregation statistic and any digitization/normalization procedure are recorded. Synthetic or schematic curves must not be presented as empirical evidence.
